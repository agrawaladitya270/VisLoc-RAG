VisLoc-RAG

Upload a photo of a landmark, building, dish or object. The app identifies it, writes facts from English and Japanese Wikipedia, and checks each fact against the source it cites. Facts that fail the check are not shown.

JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB. You can also drop a file on this page.
Optional. Where you are; used as a hint.
